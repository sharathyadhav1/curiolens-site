Privacy Policy

CurioLens Publisher · Last updated 25 October 2026

Who we are

CurioLens Publisher is an internal publishing tool operated by CurioLens Studios. It is used only by CurioLens Studios to publish CurioLens Studios' own videos to CurioLens Studios' own YouTube channel, Instagram account, Facebook Page and Threads profile. It is not offered to the public or to other businesses.

What data the app accesses

  • The names, usernames and IDs of the CurioLens Studios accounts it publishes to (YouTube channel, Facebook Page, Instagram account, Threads profile).
  • The access tokens and permissions granted by CurioLens Studios to publish to those accounts.
  • Publishing results: the IDs, links and processing status of posts the app creates.

The app does not read, collect or store data about other people, including followers, commenters or message senders.

How the data is used and stored

This data is used only to publish videos that a person at CurioLens Studios has explicitly approved, and to confirm that they were published. Access tokens are stored on CurioLens Studios' own computer, in files readable only by its operator account, and are never published, shared or included in source code.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share this data with anyone. Data is sent only to the platform that receives each post (Google/YouTube or Meta) as part of publishing.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept while CurioLens Studios uses the app and are deleted when access is revoked. See Data deletion for how to remove access and request deletion.
