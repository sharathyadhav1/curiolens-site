Data Deletion Instructions

CurioLens Publisher · Last updated 25 October 2026

CurioLens Publisher only stores account IDs, names and access tokens for CurioLens Studios' own accounts. It stores no data about other people.

Remove the app's access

  • Facebook and Instagram: Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites) → select CurioLens Publisher → Remove.
  • Threads: Threads → Settings → Account → Website permissions → remove CurioLens Publisher.
  • YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions → CurioLens Publisher → Delete all connections.

Request deletion of stored data

Email curiolens.makers@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request”. Stored tokens and account details are deleted within 30 days, and we confirm by reply.
